Support

Pocket Assistant for iPhone. iOS 26 or later.

What the app is

Pocket Assistant is a pocket manager for the things you are trying to finish. Not a habit tracker, not a to-do list, not a calendar. Every goal, the step it is on, and the times you planned. Four coaches that change what the app does, never how it talks. No scores, no streaks.

Getting help

Questions, problems and suggestions: open an issue on GitHub. Say which iPhone and iOS version you are on and what you expected to happen. A GitHub account is free.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your iPhone only. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
  • Reminders are not arriving. Check that reminders are on in the app's Settings and that notifications for Pocket Assistant are allowed in iOS Settings. Reminders are tied to the times you planned.
  • Suggested answers do not appear. They need Apple Intelligence turned on and a device that supports it. The app works fully without them, and the setting can be turned off.
  • Can I use it on iPad or Mac? The app is made for iPhone, portrait only.
  • Is there a subscription? No. The app is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no recurring charge and no in-app purchases.
  • How do I delete everything? Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it.
