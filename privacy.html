Pocket Assistant

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026. Applies to Pocket Assistant for iPhone, version 1.0 and later.

Everything stays on your iPhone

Pocket Assistant keeps everything on your device. The goals, steps and planned times you enter, your settings, your chosen coach and theme, and the first name you type at onboarding are stored in the app's own folder on your iPhone and nowhere else.

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server. It does not connect to the network, does not collect analytics or crash reports of its own, shows no ads, contains no third-party code and makes no purchases inside the app.

What the app does not collect

Nothing. No personal data, usage data, identifiers, location, contacts or diagnostics are collected by the app or sent to the developer or to anyone else. In App Store terms, the app's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. No notification service outside the device is involved. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Suggested answers and Apple Intelligence

The optional "Suggested answers" setting uses Apple Intelligence on your device to suggest wording from the goal notes you have written yourself. This runs with Apple's on-device model. The app sends nothing to Apple, to the developer or to anyone else. The feature can be turned off in Settings, and every part of the app works without it or on a device that does not support it.

Backups

The app's data is ordinary app data on your iPhone. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, it is included in that backup like any other app's data, under Apple's terms. The app itself does not use iCloud or any sync service.

Deleting your data

Deleting a goal in the app removes it, its planned times and its history for good. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything the app stored.

Purchase

Pocket Assistant is a one-time paid download from the App Store. There is no subscription, no recurring charge and no in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's terms, and the app receives no payment or account information.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It is rated 4+.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated on this page and the date at the top will change.
